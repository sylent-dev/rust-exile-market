A new version of Exile Market, a price checker for Path of Exile 2, free and open source, for Windows and Linux. It is still a pre-alpha: expect bugs, and report them through the bug icon of the title bar.

The download page: https://exile-market.sylent.tools

## What's new

- **Close with the game**: a new setting, off by default. Checked, Exile Market quits when Path of Exile 2 closes, once the game was seen running: starting the tool before the game doesn't close it.
- **Show the pseudo totals**: a new setting, on by default. Unchecked, the resistances, life, mana and attributes added up are no longer among the filters. The settings are now grouped under Game, Hotkey and Search.
- **Waystone tier range**: the tier of a waystone is now a filter of its own, "12 – 12" by default, which you can widen ("11 – 13") to see the offers of nearby tiers.
- **Orb names**: hover the orb of a price to read its name and its rate.

## What changed

- **Uncertain prices**: a price on few trades or offers says so under its title ("Low reliability: 3 trades"), and the note of an estimate gives the range of the offers it counts. An offer paid in a currency without a reliable price is shown faint, "not counted".
- **Lines without tier**: a line whose value fits several modifiers now reads "Uncertain tier", and a value no known modifier holds "No tier found". A sanctified item says why its lines have no tier, and the open places of an item read "up to 1 open" while one of its lines isn't known as a prefix or a suffix.
- **Error texts**: an answer of the price database that can't be read is no longer shown as "can't be reached", and a refusal of the trade site is no longer shown as a limit.
- **Small texts**: the DPS of a weapon tells, in a tooltip, that it counts its quality, modifiers and runes; the item class searched, when cut by "…", shows its whole text in a tooltip.

## Download

| System | Archive |
|---|---|
| Windows | `exile-market-windows-x64.zip` |
| Linux | `exile-market-linux-x64.tar.gz` |

Unpack it and start `exile-market.exe` (Windows) or `exile-market` (Linux). There is nothing to install: to update, replace the folder of the previous version, your settings are kept. The executable isn't signed for now, so Windows may show an alert at the first launch: "More info", then "Run anyway". The checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

The macOS archives (`exile-market-macos-arm64.tar.gz`, `exile-market-macos-x64.tar.gz`) are for players who run the Windows client through a compatibility layer: there, only Ctrl+C copies an item, without the window over the game nor Close with the game. They are less tested.

## Before you play

Put the game in **windowed fullscreen** or in a **window**. Exclusive fullscreen isn't supported: Ctrl+D copies nothing, and the D reaches the game.

## Known limits

- On Linux, the hotkey, the window over the game and Close with the game were tried on KDE Plasma alone. On another desktop (GNOME…), they may not behave the same: a report helps.
