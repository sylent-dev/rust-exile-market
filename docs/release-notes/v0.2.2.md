A new version of Exile Market, a price checker for Path of Exile 2, free and open source, for Windows and Linux. It is still a pre-alpha: expect bugs, and report them through the bug icon of the title bar.

The download page: https://exile-market.sylent.tools

## What's new

- **A call to estimate**: when an item is priced from similar offers (a rare, a jewel, a gem…), its price block is titled "Estimated price" from the start, with faint dashes where the amounts will go and the line "Search the offers below to estimate its price.", and the search block beats twice to show where to click. The search still starts only on a click.

## What changed

- **The limits of the trade site are kept**: what Exile Market knows of the limits of the trade site (the searches of the last hours, the 10 seconds after a search) now outlives a restart. Before, a new launch knew no limit until its first answer, and could send a search the site would refuse, which lengthens the wait.
- **The wait of the item names is kept**: when the trade site refuses the download of the item names, its hour of wait goes on after a restart too.

## What's fixed

- **Messages on KDE**: the message shown when Exile Market is already open, or when its window can't open, now keeps its line breaks and its text as written.

## Download

| System | Archive |
|---|---|
| Windows | `exile-market-windows-x64.zip` |
| Linux | `exile-market-linux-x64.tar.gz` |

Unpack it and start `exile-market.exe` (Windows) or `exile-market` (Linux). There is nothing to install: to update, replace the folder of the previous version, your settings are kept. The executable isn't signed for now, so Windows may show an alert at the first launch: "More info", then "Run anyway". The checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

The macOS archives (`exile-market-macos-arm64.tar.gz`, `exile-market-macos-x64.tar.gz`) are for players who run the Windows client through a compatibility layer: there, only Ctrl+C copies an item, without the window over the game nor Close with the game. They are less tested.

## Before you play

Put the game in **windowed fullscreen** or in a **window**. Exclusive fullscreen isn't supported: Ctrl+D copies nothing, and the D reaches the game.

## Known limits

- On Linux, the hotkey, the window over the game and Close with the game were tried on KDE Plasma alone. On another desktop (GNOME…), they may not behave the same: a report helps.
