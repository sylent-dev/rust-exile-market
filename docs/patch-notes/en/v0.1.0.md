- **New**: the first version of Exile Market. Press Ctrl+D over an item in the game to see its price in a small window over the game, or Ctrl+C to see it in the main window.
- **New**: currencies and uniques get their price at once, in three currencies.
- **New**: rare, magic and normal items and jewels show their stats and tiers. Tick the stats you care about, then search similar offers on the official trade site for an estimated price.
- **New**: the game in English or French, and the interface in both.
