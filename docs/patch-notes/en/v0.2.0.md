- **New**: a setting closes Exile Market when the game closes. Another one hides the added-up resistances, life, mana and attributes from the filters.
- **New**: search a waystone between two tiers, to see the offers of nearby tiers too.
- **New**: hover the orb of a price to read its name and its rate.
- **Changed**: a price based on few trades or offers says so, and an offer that doesn't count in the estimate is shown faint.
- **Changed**: a line without tier now says why: its tier is uncertain, or no known modifier holds its value.
- **Changed**: clearer messages when the price database answers something unreadable, and when the trade site refuses a search.
